# 12. Demo Presentation Script

Target length: 12–15 minutes.

## 1. Enterprise structure — Maya, Company Owner
- Start at My Products.
- Explain one shared account, two public products, no Company 3 front door.
- Open GD and Signatrain to show distinct navigation and data boundaries.

## 2. Company administration — Jordan, Company HR Admin
- Open Users and Seats.
- Invite a fictional employee and assign a seat.
- Show that Jordan can manage learning access but cannot see Billing.

## 3. HR learning — Elena, HR Subscriber
- Continue New HR Bootcamp.
- Demonstrate ordered modules and the 95% video rule.
- Complete the optional evaluation.
- Open the issued certificate and its public verification page.
- Register for a Masterclass and show the simulated Zoom reference.

## 4. Manager experience — Marcus, Manager Subscriber
- Show the manager-specific program and scenario library.
- Point out that HR Masterclasses, Bot, and Legislative Tracking are absent.

## 5. GD legal-service front door — Maya, GD Client User
- Show plan, benefits, and onboarding.
- Submit a restricted legal request with a fictional attachment.

## 6. GD operations — Dana then Rachel
- Dana triages and assigns the request.
- Rachel sends a client-visible message, adds an internal note, and creates a limited matter reference.
- Return to Maya to prove that the internal note is not visible.

## 7. Legislative Tracking — Sam then Rachel then Elena
- Sam opens a draft alert and submits it.
- Rachel approves and signs off.
- Publish and distribute.
- Elena receives and marks the alert read.

## 8. Commercial path — prospect persona
- Open pricing and complete a simulated Signatrain HR checkout.
- Show immediate entitlement and seat activation.

## Closing message
- This demo validates behavior and scope.
- Production architecture, integrations, security, and estimation are separate subsequent deliverables.
